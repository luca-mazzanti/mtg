# Pauper Metagame Report: 2026-10-27

Cross-check of the Pauper metagame on [DeckScout](https://deckscout.fyi/metagame/pauper) against my collection (`mtg-collection.md`).

## Scope and method

- **Source:** the DeckScout Pauper metagame page, 30-day window, 877 Challenge decks. The 20 ranked archetypes with 6+ decks are covered here (83.5% of the field; another 12.5% is unclassified). The 14 archetypes below 1% share (Caw Gates, Jeskai Control, Jeskai Ephemerate, Mono Blue Faeries, Mono Red Synth, Dimir Terror, Izzet Skred, Cycle Storm, Dredge, Poison Storm, Orzhov Bounce, Mono Black Sacrifice, Mono White Heroic, Slivers) are not analysed.
- **Cards considered:** for each archetype, the 15 most-played mainboard cards and the 15 most-played sideboard cards (Mono Blue Terror lists fewer sideboard cards as the site shows). Rarer cards are not in the site's top tables, so they are not covered.
- **Copies needed:** the site's average copies per deck, rounded to the nearest whole number (minimum 1). A card in both main and side has the two added together.
- **Ownership:** copies in the collection matched by English name, ignoring printing, language and foil. Basic lands are not tracked.
- **Coverage:** the share of those needed copies I already own.

## Caveats

- The site's averages are over decks that play the card, so the true per-deck average is slightly lower for cards with a play rate below 100%.
- Each archetype page was truncated at 100,000 characters, so only the top-card tables were used; individual decklists were not averaged.
- Ownership ignores printing, language and foil. A card owned only in Italian or foil counts as owned.
- Copies are counted per archetype. Several decks can share the same copies if built one at a time; the "Most useful cards to buy" table uses the highest need of any single archetype.
